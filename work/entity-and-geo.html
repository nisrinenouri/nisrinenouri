CASE 04 / Entity & GEO analysis

Measuring how a brand is understood.

Connecting search results, AI answers, and business profile information in a repeatable review process.

International business · Anonymous client Brand disambiguation · AI answer evaluation · Local SEO
01

The challenge

Identify inconsistent brand information and ambiguity across search results and AI-generated answers in multiple markets.

02

My contribution

I adapted an employer-developed evaluation framework to the project, reviewed brand clarity and answer accuracy, and organized recurring analysis. I managed business profile verification and information updates, prepared implementation guidance, and tracked delivery.

03

Observed outcome

The follow-up report records improved evaluation results in most reviewed markets, with one market declining. The improvement was primarily associated with business profile updates; it should not be presented as a universal improvement in AI answer quality.

04

Methodology and ownership

This case describes my analytical and coordination role. The proprietary framework, score values, formulas, market combinations, and internal documents are not reproduced.

About this case study

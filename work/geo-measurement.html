IN-HOUSE MARKETING / CONTENT STRATEGY & GEO

Turning search demand into content direction.

Search-intent analysis, AI citation research, and an inbound content plan connecting audience questions with service content.

Marketing · In-house project Google Search Console · Peec AI · Content strategy
01

Use search behaviour to inform strategy

I analyzed Search Console queries to understand audience questions and service-related intent. I used that research to organize the topics that mattered for the business.

02

Connect SEO research with AI search analysis

I reviewed which content AI systems referenced and compared it with the services the business wanted to be known for. The analysis informed recommendations for content formats and topic coverage.

03

Build a content plan around the customer journey

I developed an inbound content map with pillar pages, supporting topic clusters, decision-stage content, and service pages. The plan connected industry-specific questions with relevant calls to action.

04

My contribution

Search-intent research, topic mapping, AI citation analysis, and content planning. The delivered work was the analysis and strategy used to guide content production.

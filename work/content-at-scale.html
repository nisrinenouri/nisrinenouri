CASE 02 / Content & technical SEO

Making a large content library easier to find.

A sustained optimization programme spanning hundreds of pages, technical issue tracking, and search performance reporting.

Professional content platform · Anonymous client On-page SEO · Technical QA · Analytics
01

The challenge

Improve a substantial content library while working through access-dependent errors, URL inconsistencies, and indexing issues.

02

What I delivered

I optimized page titles, metadata, and content in recurring batches; maintained delivery tracking; reviewed indexing; and prepared weekly and monthly reports. I identified technical problems and coordinated fixes with the development partner.

03

Observed outcomes

The reports record completion of the page optimization scope, higher year-over-year search clicks and impressions, and fewer audit errors. These were project-level outcomes involving content work, technical changes, and wider team contributions.

04

AI search evidence

A supplied Google AI Overview capture shows a client resource among its cited sources. This documents one observed citation, not a sustained citation rate or a causal effect of a specific optimization.

About this case study

Based on private work reports and, where shown, supplied result captures. Visuals are original anonymized reconstructions. Client names, URLs, exact project dates, and identifying assets are withheld.

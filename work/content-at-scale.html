Healthcare content / SEO growth & content operations

500+ pages. Stronger organic search performance.

I delivered a large-scale content SEO project and coordinated technical improvements. Project reporting recorded approximately 280% more search clicks and 730% more impressions year over year.

Healthcare content Ahrefs · Google Search Console · GA4 · Looker Studio
01

A content library with room to grow

The healthcare platform needed consistent optimization across a large library of specialist resources, alongside technical improvements that helped search engines access and understand the content.

02

My role: content execution and technical coordination

I optimized titles, meta descriptions, and page content in weekly batches, maintained the delivery dashboard, checked indexing, and prepared weekly and monthly reporting. I identified URL inconsistencies, redirects, and access-related errors and worked with the development partner through resolution.

03

Delivery at scale

I completed the SEO work across 500+ pages and increased weekly output from 10 to 15 pages to meet the project schedule. I connected Search Console, GA4, Ahrefs, and Looker Studio reporting to communicate search performance, technical progress, and audience behaviour.

04

Search growth and technical improvement

Comparable year-over-year reporting windows showed approximately 280% growth in clicks and 730% growth in impressions. Average search position improved by 2.6 positions. A separate crawl comparison recorded a 99% reduction in audit errors and an Ahrefs Health Score of 100.

05

AI visibility and off-page delivery

A supplied Google AI Overview capture shows a client resource cited as a supporting source. I also coordinated three off-page specialists, reviewed placements and editorial deliverables, and tracked publication and follow-up.

CASE 01 / Technical implementation

From audit findings to verified fixes.

Hands-on technical SEO fixes, schema implementation, and llms.txt delivery on a Webflow website.

Technology company · Anonymous client Webflow · Structured data · Crawl validation
01

The challenge

Turn technical audit findings into implemented changes and validate the resulting website.

02

My contribution

I fixed the identified technical issues and implemented schema markup and llms.txt. I checked the resulting crawl and structured-data validation outputs.

03

What the evidence shows

The supplied Ahrefs snapshot records a Health Score of 100. The displayed WebSite item in the Schema.org validator reports zero errors and zero warnings.

04

What this does not establish

These are point-in-time technical checks. They do not prove that every possible issue is absent, that Google will show a rich result, or that llms.txt caused an increase in AI visibility.

About this case study

Based on private work reports and, where shown, supplied result captures. Visuals are original anonymized reconstructions. Client names, URLs, exact project dates, and identifying assets are withheld.

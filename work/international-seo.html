CASE 03 / International SEO

Consistent search content across languages.

Combining direct content optimization with multilingual quality assurance and implementation coordination.

Global B2B company · Anonymous client English & French · Localization QA · Delivery coordination
01

Direct execution

I completed English SEO work and French translation and SEO for product and category pages.

02

Coordinated delivery

I recruited and coordinated language specialists, created working guidelines, reviewed deliverables, managed revision requests, and maintained language-level delivery tracking.

03

Quality checks

The work covered content consistency, product terminology, metadata, and image alternative text. I also documented technical issues and priorities for the implementation team.

04

Outcome and limits

The reports document completed content work and a repeatable review process. They do not establish a comparable traffic uplift for every language. Language coordination is separate from personal fluency or direct translation.

About this case study

Based on private work reports and, where shown, supplied result captures. Visuals are original anonymized reconstructions. Client names, URLs, exact project dates, and identifying assets are withheld.

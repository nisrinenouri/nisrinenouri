CASE 05 / WordPress implementation

Turning technical findings into practical repairs.

Direct WordPress fixes supported by recrawling, issue triage, and clear developer handoffs.

Professional services business · Anonymous client WordPress · Redirects · Metadata
01

Hands-on work

I investigated and corrected redirect and 404 issues, added robots.txt, addressed duplicate schema and meta tags, and improved image alternative text on the WordPress blog.

02

Verification and triage

I used follow-up crawls and Search Console data to review the site and distinguish current issues from historical URL records.

03

Working across platforms

For the separate Framer site, I documented technical constraints and prepared an implementation guide for the development team. This was a developer handoff rather than a claim of direct Framer implementation.

04

Outcome

The reports document technical corrections and a structured follow-up process. No unsupported revenue, traffic, or automation time-saving figures are attached to this work.
